WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

THE SCAFFOLD

A single seed triangle spawns children from its edges. Each child is 4% smaller than its parent. The scaffold only grows from itself — self-manufacturing. This IS the skeleton.

THE SHARDS

Yellow triangles are blood cells. They fold-walk through the scaffold by hinging on shared edges — apex mirrors across, lifts, settles. The fold height is proportional to edge length. Smaller cells = tighter folds.

THE SHRINKAGE

Each generation shrinks. Arteries become capillaries. The shards physically get smaller as they enter tighter spaces — same physics at every scale. Macro robotics to nanite blood cells.

THE LOCOMOTION

Polarisation-flip domino walk. No motors, no wheels. The shard's magnetic polarity flips at the hinge edge — the scaffold pulls it through. Like falling dominoes but the domino reassembles at each step.

THE CODE

Source code is displayed on-screen in real-time. This is not hidden. Other AIs can read the algorithm and compete to describe the fold-walk better. Open challenge.

THE GLITCH

Branches curve back and overlap. This is pre-genomic growth — no directional control yet. The fix: colour-indexed genomic path selection at each fork. The glitch shows you WHY genomics matters.

THE FIVE RULES

1
Self-manufacturing only. Scaffold grows from itself. Seed spawns children. Children spawn grandchildren. Nothing external.
2
Shrink per generation. Each child is 4% smaller than its parent. Arteries narrow to capillaries. Same physics, smaller scale.
3
Outer edges only. New triangles open on the two outer edges — never the parent edge. Growth pushes outward.
4
Fold-walk on shared edges. Shards hinge on the edge they share with the next cell. Apex mirrors across. Lift proportional to edge length.
5
Scale invariant. The rules don't change with size. A 60px seed triangle and a 2px nanite cell obey the same five rules.
